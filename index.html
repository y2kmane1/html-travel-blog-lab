Adventures in Fire Island

Trip Summary

My trip to Fire Island National Seashore was a perfect escape from the city. I walked along quiet beaches, climbed the historic lighthouse for sweeping views, and wandered through the Sunken Forest, a rare maritime holly woodland tucked behind the dunes.

Highlights included spotting deer along the boardwalk trails, watching the sunset over the Atlantic, and meeting the friendly locals in the car-free villages.
